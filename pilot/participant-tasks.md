# Ultimo pilot: participant task card

We are testing the application, not you. This session uses fictional information. You can pause, skip a task or stop. Your reading marks and self-checks do not certify competence. Please say what you expect to happen and where anything is confusing; asking for help is useful feedback.

Use your usual phone or computer browser. A facilitator will prepare a clean test profile and restore the fictional starter record before you begin. If you use a screen reader or keyboard, use your normal setup. The app is draft teaching material.

1. **Choose a plan.** Choose the Mental faculty with five hours per week. Find which courses it includes. Try another plan, return to Mental and reload the page.
2. **Find a lesson.** Find INT-101, *Learning how to learn*, and open its first lesson. Find its objective, worked example and knowledge check. Return to the course, then reopen the lesson.
3. **Try the self-check.** Type the fictional answer “A short practice task with feedback”. Try saving before revealing all answers. Then reveal each answer, mark one as matching and save. Explain what the recorded result means.
4. **Mark the lesson read.** Record L01 as read. Find the reading and review dates. Explain whether this proves competence.
5. **Complete an earlier review.** Find the due review for INT-101.L02 from the fictional starter record. Open it, try a recall, record the review and find its next date.
6. **Save a note.** Save this for INT-101: “Fictional pilot: compared two practice plans, tried a short retrieval task and noted one change.” Find the note, cancel deleting it, then delete it. Save it again for the next task.
7. **Transfer a backup.** Download your test records. Change the study plan, then restore the downloaded backup. First cancel replacement, then try again and accept it. Check which records returned.
8. **Erase the test records.** First cancel erasure, then confirm it. Reload and check the result. Delete your test downloads when the facilitator asks.

Afterwards, tell the facilitator:

- Which task was hardest to find or understand?
- Did anything happen differently from what you expected?
- What would make this easier on your device?
- What does the app's draft status and local-only storage mean to you?

Keep personal information out of notes and quiz attempts. The facilitator records anonymous observations in a private file, with your consent. A separate later learning exercise can test comprehension over time; this walkthrough does not establish learning effectiveness.
