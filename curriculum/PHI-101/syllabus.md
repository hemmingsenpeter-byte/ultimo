# PHI-101 — Ancient Greek philosophy

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [INT-102 — Logic and argument](../INT-102/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Socratic inquiry

- **Outcome:** Explain socratic inquiry in the context of ancient greek philosophy.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving socratic inquiry; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to comparative philosophy essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain socratic inquiry from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of socratic inquiry; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-101.L01`

### L02 — Plato

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish plato in the context of ancient greek philosophy.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving plato; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

**Artifact:** Comparative philosophy essay.

All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.
