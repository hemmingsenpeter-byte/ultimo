# MEN-106 — Stress management

Framework defined; lesson content: **draft**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [EMO-101 — Emotional identification](../EMO-101/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Acute and chronic stress

- **Outcome:** Explain acute and chronic stress in the context of stress management.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving acute and chronic stress; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to demand-resource audit.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain acute and chronic stress from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of acute and chronic stress; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `MEN-106.L01`

### L02 — Demands and resources

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish demands and resources in the context of stress management.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving demands and resources; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to demand-resource audit.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain demands and resources from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of demands and resources; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `MEN-106.L02`

### L03 — Coping choices

- **Outcome:** Apply coping choices in the context of stress management.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving coping choices; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to demand-resource audit.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain coping choices from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of coping choices; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `MEN-106.L03`

### L04 — Escalation

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about escalation in the context of stress management.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving escalation; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to demand-resource audit.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain escalation from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of escalation; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `MEN-106.L04`
