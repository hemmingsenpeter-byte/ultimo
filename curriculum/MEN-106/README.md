# MEN-106 — Stress management

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain acute and chronic stress in the context of stress management.
- Compare and distinguish demands and resources in the context of stress management.
- Apply coping choices in the context of stress management.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about escalation in the context of stress management.
