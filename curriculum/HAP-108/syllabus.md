## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Life domains

- **Outcome:** Explain life domains in the context of life design.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving life domains; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to three life plans.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain life domains from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of life domains; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `HAP-108.L01`

### L02 — Alternative futures

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish alternative futures in the context of life design.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving alternative futures; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to three life plans.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain alternative futures from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of alternative futures; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `HAP-108.L02`

### L03 — Reversible experiments

- **Outcome:** Apply reversible experiments in the context of life design.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving reversible experiments; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to three life plans.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain reversible experiments from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of reversible experiments; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `HAP-108.L03`

### L04 — Trade-offs

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about trade-offs in the context of life design.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving trade-offs; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to three life plans.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain trade-offs from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of trade-offs; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `HAP-108.L04`
