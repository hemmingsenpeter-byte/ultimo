# ART-101 — Practical assessment

Draft; use private or fictional evidence.

## Capstone

Produce four observational studies or annotated descriptions: negative space, proportion, three-value composition and revised drawing.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Observation and negative space | Explains the idea accurately | Identifies contours and negative shapes | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L02: Proportion | Explains the idea accurately | demonstrates relative measurement | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L03: Tone and composition | Explains the idea accurately | arranges value with explicit intent | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L04: Critique | Explains the idea accurately | critiques with evidence and testable revision | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; do not average away missing or safety-critical criteria. Knowledge checks are formative. Level 3 needs delayed independent evidence and appropriate review. Higher levels require repeated application and qualified judgement.

## Feedback

Submit artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next practice action. Retest the unmet criterion. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
