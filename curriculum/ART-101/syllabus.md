# ART-101 — Drawing and observation

Framework defined; lesson content: **draft**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice; Level 3 extension; 6 indicative study hours. [Read the course](README.md).

## Prerequisites
None.

## Lesson blueprints and authored materials
### [L01 — Observation and negative space](L01.md)

- **Outcome:** Explain observation and negative space in the context of drawing and observation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving observation and negative space; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to drawing portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain observation and negative space from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of observation and negative space; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-101.L01`

## Assessment
Drawing portfolio. All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.

**Review:** Practising artist or arts educator; cultural expertise where relevant.. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
