# ART-101 — Drawing and observation

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Observation and negative space

- **Outcome:** Explain observation and negative space in the context of drawing and observation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving observation and negative space; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to drawing portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain observation and negative space from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of observation and negative space; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-101.L01`

### L02 — Proportion

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish proportion in the context of drawing and observation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving proportion; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to drawing portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain proportion from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of proportion; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-101.L02`

### L03 — Tone and composition

- **Outcome:** Apply tone and composition in the context of drawing and observation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving tone and composition; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to drawing portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain tone and composition from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of tone and composition; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-101.L03`

### L04 — Critique

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about critique in the context of drawing and observation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving critique; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to drawing portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain critique from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of critique; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-101.L04`

**Artifact:** Drawing portfolio.

All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.

**Review:** Practising artist or arts educator; cultural expertise where relevant.. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
