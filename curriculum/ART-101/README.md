# ART-101 — Drawing and observation

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours, excluding extended practice. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain observation and negative space in the context of drawing and observation.
- Compare and distinguish proportion in the context of drawing and observation.
- Apply tone and composition in the context of drawing and observation.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about critique in the context of drawing and observation.
