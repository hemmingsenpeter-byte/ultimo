# INT-104 — Practical assessment

Draft; use fictional evidence.

## Capstone

Design a fictional experiment comparing two document-filing conventions. Submit a question, operational hypothesis, outcome definition, comparison and allocation procedure, causal diagram, competing explanations and a repeatability packet. Use synthetic data or a paper simulation only. Interpret two possible outcomes and explain whether a second study would be a reproduction or replication.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Hypotheses | Defines the central distinction accurately | States a testable hypothesis, operational measurement, rival explanation and contrary result. | Applies criterion in an unfamiliar case without prompts, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L02: Controls and confounding | Defines the central distinction accurately | Identifies a confounder and proposes a defensible comparison with measurement safeguards. | Applies criterion in an unfamiliar case without prompts, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L03: Causal inference | Defines the central distinction accurately | Articulates a counterfactual, alternative causal paths, study design assumptions and bounded conclusion. | Applies criterion in an unfamiliar case without prompts, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L04: Replication | Defines the central distinction accurately | Produces a usable repeatability packet and interprets agreement or discrepancy without overclaiming. | Applies criterion in an unfamiliar case without prompts, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative only.

## Feedback

Submit fictional artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next action. Retest unmet criteria. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual or simulated evidence is accepted. Independent source and subject review pending.
