# PHY-106 — Self-defence and de-escalation

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Environmental awareness

- **Outcome:** Explain environmental awareness in the context of self-defence and de-escalation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving environmental awareness; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to non-contact scenario plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain environmental awareness from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of environmental awareness; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-106.L01`

### L02 — Verbal de-escalation

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish verbal de-escalation in the context of self-defence and de-escalation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving verbal de-escalation; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to non-contact scenario plan.

### L03 — Escape and assistance

- **Outcome:** Apply escape and assistance in the context of self-defence and de-escalation.
