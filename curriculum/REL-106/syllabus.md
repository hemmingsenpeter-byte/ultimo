## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Visible and invisible labour

- **Outcome:** Explain visible and invisible labour in the context of long-term partnership.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving visible and invisible labour; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to household partnership plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain visible and invisible labour from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of visible and invisible labour; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `REL-106.L01`

### L02 — Connection routines

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish connection routines in the context of long-term partnership.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving connection routines; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to household partnership plan.
