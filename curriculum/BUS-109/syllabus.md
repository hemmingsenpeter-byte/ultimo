## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Strategic diagnosis

- **Outcome:** Explain strategic diagnosis in the context of entrepreneurship and strategy.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving strategic diagnosis; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to venture strategy memo.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain strategic diagnosis from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of strategic diagnosis; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BUS-109.L01`

### L02 — Competitive options

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish competitive options in the context of entrepreneurship and strategy.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving competitive options; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
