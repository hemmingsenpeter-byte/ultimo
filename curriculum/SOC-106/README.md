# SOC-106 — Group dynamics

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain roles and norms in the context of group dynamics.
- Compare and distinguish conformity in the context of group dynamics.
- Apply facilitation in the context of group dynamics.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about inclusion in the context of group dynamics.
