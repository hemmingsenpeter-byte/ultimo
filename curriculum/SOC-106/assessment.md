# SOC-106 — Practical assessment

Draft — independent review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Develop a fictional meeting facilitation plan with role and authority map, conformity risk and counterevidence procedure, timed decision agenda and inclusion audit. Record decision, actions, owners, deadlines and unresolved constraints; include accessible alternatives, dissent and revised plan following feedback.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Roles and norms | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Maps roles, responsibilities, authority and dependencies while distinguishing status and informal norms. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Conformity | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Distinguishes social influence mechanisms, evaluates evidence behind consensus and designs safe opportunities for dissent. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Facilitation | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Designs a decision-oriented, accessible meeting process with clear authority, actions and review. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Inclusion | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Identifies barriers and verifies equivalent participation routes, consent and responsibility for unresolved access issues. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative only; no personality or popularity assessment.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest unmet criteria on a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, manipulation, coercion or certification. Independent source, subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
