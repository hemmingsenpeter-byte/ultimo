# ART-102 — Music

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Rhythm melody harmony

- **Outcome:** Explain rhythm melody harmony in the context of music.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving rhythm melody harmony; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to listening and composition study.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain rhythm melody harmony from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of rhythm melody harmony; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-102.L01`

### L02 — Musical form
