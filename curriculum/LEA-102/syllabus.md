# LEA-102 — Team dynamics

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 3; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Team purpose

- **Outcome:** Explain team purpose in the context of team dynamics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving team purpose; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to team charter.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain team purpose from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of team purpose; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `LEA-102.L01`

### L02 — Role clarity

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish role clarity in the context of team dynamics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving role clarity; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to team charter.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain role clarity from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of role clarity; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `LEA-102.L02`

### L03 — Psychological safety

- **Outcome:** Apply psychological safety in the context of team dynamics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving psychological safety; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to team charter.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain psychological safety from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.

### L04 — Feedback

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about feedback in the context of team dynamics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving feedback; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of feedback; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `LEA-102.L04`
