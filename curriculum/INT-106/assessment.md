# INT-106 — Practical assessment

Draft; fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Model a fictional community repair service. Specify backlog stock and flows over four periods, two signed feedback loops, an implementation delay, and three possible intervention points. Test one scenario with arithmetic, compare intervention trade-offs and propose monitoring guardrails and a reversal condition.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Stocks and flows | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Builds a dimensionally consistent stock-flow balance and interprets net changes. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L02: Feedback loops | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Constructs signed closed loops and explains their hypothesised behaviour and limits. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L03: Delays | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Models a delay explicitly and compares responses across time. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L04: Intervention points | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Justifies a bounded intervention using mechanisms, alternatives and guardrails. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quizzes are formative; self-recorded work is not certification.

## Feedback

Provide a fictional artifact, reasoning, assumptions and corrections. Reviewer identifies one strength, one gap and a specific next task. Retest unmet criteria. Equivalent accessible written, spoken, diagrammatic or simulated evidence is accepted. Independent source, subject and learner review required.
