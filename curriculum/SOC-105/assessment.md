# SOC-105 — Practical assessment

Draft — independent review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Produce a fictional introduction and feedback portfolio: attentive welcome, context-sensitive humour decision, truthful short story and authentic role-appropriate introduction. Include a recipient refusal, factual correction, privacy boundary and revised attempt. Assess accuracy, relevance, respect and follow-through, not charm or popularity.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Warmth and attention | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Demonstrates accurate attention, proportionate warmth, role clarity and respect for refusal without inferring character. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Humour | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Evaluates humour by target, purpose, context and impact, and chooses respectful alternatives and repair. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Engaging stories | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Constructs an accurate, coherent, bounded narrative without presenting anecdotes as general proof or exposing private information. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Authenticity | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Communicates truthful capabilities and values with contextual adaptation, privacy and realistic commitments. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative only; no personality or popularity assessment.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest unmet criteria on a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, manipulation, coercion or certification. Independent source, subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
