# SOC-105 — Charisma and humour

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain warmth and attention in the context of charisma and humour.
- Compare and distinguish humour in the context of charisma and humour.
- Apply engaging stories in the context of charisma and humour.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about authenticity in the context of charisma and humour.
