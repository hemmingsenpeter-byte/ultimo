# ART-106 — Creative writing

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 3; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [COM-102 — Writing](../COM-102/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Character and scene

- **Outcome:** Explain character and scene in the context of creative writing.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving character and scene; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to original story or essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain character and scene from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of character and scene; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-106.L01`

### L02 — Narrative voice

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish narrative voice in the context of creative writing.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving narrative voice; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to original story or essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain narrative voice from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of narrative voice; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-106.L02`

### L03 — Imagery and dialogue

- **Outcome:** Apply imagery and dialogue in the context of creative writing.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving imagery and dialogue; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to original story or essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain imagery and dialogue from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of imagery and dialogue; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-106.L03`

### L04 — Editing

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about editing in the context of creative writing.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving editing; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to original story or essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain editing from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of editing; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-106.L04`

## Assessment
**Artifact:** Original story or essay.

All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.

**Review:** Practising artist or arts educator; cultural expertise where relevant.. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
