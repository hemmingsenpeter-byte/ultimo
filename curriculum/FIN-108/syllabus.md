## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Opportunity cost

- **Outcome:** Explain opportunity cost in the context of capital allocation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving opportunity cost; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to capital allocation memo.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain opportunity cost from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of opportunity cost; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `FIN-108.L01`

### L02 — Risk-adjusted options

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish risk-adjusted options in the context of capital allocation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving risk-adjusted options; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to capital allocation memo.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain risk-adjusted options from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of risk-adjusted options; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `FIN-108.L02`

### L03 — Liquidity

- **Outcome:** Apply liquidity in the context of capital allocation.

### L04 — Allocation principles

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about allocation principles in the context of capital allocation.
