# SOC-101 — Practical assessment

Draft; use private or fictional evidence.

## Capstone

Write or role-play a voluntary five-minute conversation including open prompt, follow-up, reciprocal contribution, repair and optional closing invitation.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Open questions | Explains the idea accurately | Uses relevant open and follow-up questions | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L02: Turn-taking | Explains the idea accurately | allows reciprocal turns | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L03: Conversational repair | Explains the idea accurately | clarifies misunderstanding without blame | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L04: Closing respectfully | Explains the idea accurately | closes clearly and respects refusal | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; do not average away missing or safety-critical criteria. Knowledge checks are formative. Level 3 needs delayed independent evidence and appropriate review. Higher levels require repeated application and qualified judgement.

## Feedback

Submit artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next practice action. Retest the unmet criterion. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
