# SOC-101 — Conversation

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours, excluding extended practice. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain open questions in the context of conversation.
- Compare and distinguish turn-taking in the context of conversation.
- Apply conversational repair in the context of conversation.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about closing respectfully in the context of conversation.
