# LEA-103 — Delegation

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 4; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [LEA-102 — Team dynamics](../LEA-102/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Outcomes and decision rights

- **Outcome:** Explain outcomes and decision rights in the context of delegation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving outcomes and decision rights; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to delegation brief.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain outcomes and decision rights from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of outcomes and decision rights; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `LEA-103.L01`

### L02 — Capacity

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish capacity in the context of delegation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving capacity; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to delegation brief.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain capacity from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.

### L03 — Checkpoints

- **Outcome:** Apply checkpoints in the context of delegation.

### L04 — Escalation

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about escalation in the context of delegation.
