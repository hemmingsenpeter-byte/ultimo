# PHY-102 — Strength and hypertrophy

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [PHY-101 — Anatomy and biomechanics](../PHY-101/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Strength and size

- **Outcome:** Explain strength and size in the context of strength and hypertrophy.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving strength and size; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to fictional training log.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain strength and size from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of strength and size; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-102.L01`

### L02 — Load and volume

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish load and volume in the context of strength and hypertrophy.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving load and volume; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to fictional training log.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain load and volume from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of load and volume; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-102.L02`

### L03 — Recovery

- **Outcome:** Apply recovery in the context of strength and hypertrophy.

### L04 — Progression

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about progression in the context of strength and hypertrophy.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of progression; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-102.L04`
