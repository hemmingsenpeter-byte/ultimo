# PHI-105 — Ethics

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [INT-102 — Logic and argument](../INT-102/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Consequences duties virtues

- **Outcome:** Explain consequences duties virtues in the context of ethics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving consequences duties virtues; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to ethical decision memo.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain consequences duties virtues from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of consequences duties virtues; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-105.L01`

### L02 — Facts and values

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish facts and values in the context of ethics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving facts and values; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to ethical decision memo.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain facts and values from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of facts and values; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-105.L02`

### L03 — Rights and interests

- **Outcome:** Apply rights and interests in the context of ethics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving rights and interests; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to ethical decision memo.

### L04 — Ethical revision

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about ethical revision in the context of ethics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving ethical revision; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

- **Knowledge check:** Explain ethical revision from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of ethical revision; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-105.L04`
