# PRA-102 — First aid

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Scene safety

- **Outcome:** Explain scene safety in the context of first aid.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving scene safety; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to emergency scenario and training referral.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain scene safety from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of scene safety; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-102.L01`

### L02 — Emergency escalation

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish emergency escalation in the context of first aid.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving emergency escalation; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to emergency scenario and training referral.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain emergency escalation from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of emergency escalation; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-102.L02`

### L03 — Official first-aid resources

- **Outcome:** Apply official first-aid resources in the context of first aid.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving official first-aid resources; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to emergency scenario and training referral.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain official first-aid resources from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of official first-aid resources; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-102.L03`

### L04 — Accredited training

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about accredited training in the context of first aid.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving accredited training; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to emergency scenario and training referral.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain accredited training from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of accredited training; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-102.L04`

## Assessment
**Artifact:** Emergency scenario and training referral.

All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.

**Review:** qualified practical safety reviewer. Draft educational framework only. Use fictional cases; no personal diagnosis, prescription, hazardous practice, financial/legal instruction or unqualified certification. Obtain qualified/local review before publication.
