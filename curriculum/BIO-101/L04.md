## Core teaching

Homeostasis is regulation of internal conditions within functional ranges, not absolute constancy. Negative feedback counteracts deviations: a sensor detects change, a coordinating process compares or responds, and effectors alter the condition. Positive feedback amplifies a process until a terminating event, as in some physiological cascades. Real regulation is distributed, with multiple interacting signals, and a measured value cannot diagnose a disease without clinical context.

## Framework

Variable → sensor → coordination → effector → changed variable. In negative feedback the response opposes the deviation.

This sequence is an instructional model, not a universal law. Check its limitations against the contrasting case.

## Worked example

When body temperature rises, regulatory responses may increase heat loss through sweating and changes in skin blood flow. Evaporation helps cool the body, while humidity and other conditions affect effectiveness.

### Contrasting case

A thermostat is a helpful analogy, but the body has interacting regulatory systems rather than a single mechanical switch.
