## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Cells and tissues | Explains the idea accurately | Distinguishes structural levels and tissues | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L02: Circulation and respiration | Explains the idea accurately | traces pulmonary and systemic circulation | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L03: Digestion and kidneys | Explains the idea accurately | distinguishes absorption from excretion | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L04: Homeostasis | Explains the idea accurately | labels negative feedback components | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; do not average away missing or safety-critical criteria. Knowledge checks are formative. Level 3 needs delayed independent evidence and appropriate review. Higher levels require repeated application and qualified judgement.

## Feedback

Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required. Draft educational framework only. Use fictional cases; no personal diagnosis, prescription, hazardous practice, financial/legal instruction or unqualified certification. Obtain qualified/local review before publication.
