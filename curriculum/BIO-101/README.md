# BIO-101 — Human biology

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours, excluding extended practice. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain cells and tissues in the context of human biology.
- Compare and distinguish circulation and respiration in the context of human biology.
- Apply digestion and kidneys in the context of human biology.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about homeostasis in the context of human biology.
