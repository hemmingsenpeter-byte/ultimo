# PRA-107 — Gardening

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Soil light water

- **Outcome:** Explain soil light water in the context of gardening.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving soil light water; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to container garden plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain soil light water from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of soil light water; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-107.L01`
