# SOC-107 — Practical assessment

Draft — independent review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Produce an event checklist for a fictional professional or community gathering. Compare etiquette across settings, design functional and accessible presentation, plan the participant journey with budget and capacity, and identify how to verify unfamiliar norms. Include role owners, confirmed versus unknown conditions, a safety or consent exception, contingency and revised checklist after feedback. No appearance scoring or personal disclosure.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Contextual etiquette | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Distinguishes requirements, conventions and preferences, and selects context-sensitive conduct respecting autonomy and safety. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Presentation | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Designs functional, truthful and accessible presentation without irrelevant appearance or status judgments. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Hosting | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Plans hosting across the participant journey, with realistic budget, access verification, responsibilities and contingency. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Unfamiliar norms | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Verifies unfamiliar norms respectfully, adapts to actual context and handles corrections and consent or safety conflicts. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no popularity, appearance or personality assessment.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest unmet criteria on a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No private disclosure, coercion, unwanted contact or certification. Independent evidence, subject, cultural, safety, accessibility and learner review required.
