# SOC-107 — Etiquette and presentation

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain contextual etiquette in the context of etiquette and presentation.
- Compare and distinguish presentation in the context of etiquette and presentation.
- Apply hosting in the context of etiquette and presentation.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about unfamiliar norms in the context of etiquette and presentation.
