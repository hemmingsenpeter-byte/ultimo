# EMO-101 — Emotional identification

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours, excluding extended practice. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain emotion labels in the context of emotional identification.
- Compare and distinguish event and interpretation in the context of emotional identification.
- Apply needs in the context of emotional identification.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about response choices in the context of emotional identification.
