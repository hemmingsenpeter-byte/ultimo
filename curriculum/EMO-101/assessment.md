# EMO-101 — Practical assessment

Draft; use private or fictional evidence.

## Capstone

Map four fictional scenarios: criticism, uncertainty, disappointment and a boundary. Include one where safety or help is preferable.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Emotion labels | Explains the idea accurately | Uses tentative labels without diagnosis | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L02: Event and interpretation | Explains the idea accurately | separates event interpretation feeling and behaviour | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L03: Needs | Explains the idea accurately | states a concern without control | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L04: Response choices | Explains the idea accurately | selects a respectful or safety-first response | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; do not average away missing or safety-critical criteria. Knowledge checks are formative. Level 3 needs delayed independent evidence and appropriate review. Higher levels require repeated application and qualified judgement.

## Feedback

Submit artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next practice action. Retest the unmet criterion. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required. Draft educational framework only. Use fictional cases; no personal diagnosis, prescription, hazardous practice, financial/legal instruction or unqualified certification. Obtain qualified/local review before publication.
