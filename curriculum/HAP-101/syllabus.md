# HAP-101 — Science of wellbeing

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [INT-104 — Scientific reasoning](../INT-104/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Affect satisfaction meaning

- **Outcome:** Explain affect satisfaction meaning in the context of science of wellbeing.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving affect satisfaction meaning; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to research critique.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain affect satisfaction meaning from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of affect satisfaction meaning; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `HAP-101.L01`
