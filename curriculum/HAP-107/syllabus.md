# HAP-107 — Nature and exploration

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Nature and evidence

- **Outcome:** Explain nature and evidence in the context of nature and exploration.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving nature and evidence; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to accessible nature plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain nature and evidence from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of nature and evidence; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `HAP-107.L01`

### L02 — Safety and access

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish safety and access in the context of nature and exploration.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving safety and access; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
