# INT-102 — Practical assessment

Draft; use fictional evidence.

## Capstone

A fictional council must decide whether to extend a library’s opening hours. Produce a two-sided argument map: annotate premises and bridges, test a deductive subargument, critique a weak inference from survey evidence, represent both sides fairly and identify a decision crux. Deliver a provisional judgement, revision condition and limitations.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Premises and conclusions | Defines the central distinction accurately | Correctly identifies claims, supporting premises and a plausible marked bridge. | Applies criterion in an unfamiliar case without prompts, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L02: Validity and soundness | Defines the central distinction accurately | Tests deductive form with a countermodel and independently assesses premise truth. | Applies criterion in an unfamiliar case without prompts, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L03: Induction and fallacies | Defines the central distinction accurately | Evaluates sampling and alternative explanations, explains a fallacy and revises the conclusion. | Applies criterion in an unfamiliar case without prompts, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L04: Steelmanning | Defines the central distinction accurately | Represents both sides faithfully, identifies the crux and specifies a revision condition. | Applies criterion in an unfamiliar case without prompts, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative only.

## Feedback

Submit fictional artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next action. Retest unmet criteria. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual or simulated evidence is accepted. Independent source and subject review pending.
