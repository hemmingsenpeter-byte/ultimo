# INT-102 — Logic and argument

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain premises and conclusions in the context of logic and argument.
- Compare and distinguish validity and soundness in the context of logic and argument.
- Apply induction and fallacies in the context of logic and argument.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about steelmanning in the context of logic and argument.
