# SOC-103 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Develop a fictional community plan: two sustainable recurring settings, a reciprocal-support and obligations analysis, two clear inclusive invitations, and a repair process addressing a documented breach or access barrier. Include cost, access, consent, refusal, legitimate duties, a formal support alternative and a review point. No popularity goals or forced contact.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Recurring contact | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Designs sustainable opportunities for recurring contact and distinguishes opportunity from mutual friendship outcomes. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Reciprocity | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Distinguishes mutual support, explicit obligations and coercive repayment demands; proposes sustainable arrangements. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Inclusive invitations | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Writes clear, accessible, voluntary invitations with transparent logistics and proportionate follow-up. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Repair | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Designs feasible community repair addressing actual impact, process changes, autonomy and appropriate escalation. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent performance and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no assessment of personal traits, popularity or clinical competence.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and one corrective task; retest unmet criteria using a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, real-person profiling, coercion, clinical treatment or certification. Independent evidence, qualified subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
