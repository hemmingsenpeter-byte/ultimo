# SOC-103 — Friendship and community

Framework defined; lesson content: **draft**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Recurring contact

- **Outcome:** Explain recurring contact in the context of friendship and community.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving recurring contact; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to community plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain recurring contact from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of recurring contact; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `SOC-103.L01`

### L02 — Reciprocity

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish reciprocity in the context of friendship and community.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving reciprocity; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to community plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain reciprocity from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of reciprocity; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.

### L03 — Inclusive invitations

- **Outcome:** Apply inclusive invitations in the context of friendship and community.
