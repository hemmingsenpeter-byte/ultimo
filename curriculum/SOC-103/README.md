# SOC-103 — Friendship and community

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain recurring contact in the context of friendship and community.
- Compare and distinguish reciprocity in the context of friendship and community.
- Apply inclusive invitations in the context of friendship and community.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about repair in the context of friendship and community.
