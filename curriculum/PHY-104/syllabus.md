# PHY-104 — Mobility and injury prevention

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [PHY-101 — Anatomy and biomechanics](../PHY-101/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Mobility and stability

- **Outcome:** Explain mobility and stability in the context of mobility and injury prevention.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving mobility and stability; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to movement decision tree.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain mobility and stability from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of mobility and stability; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-104.L01`

### L02 — Load tolerance

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish load tolerance in the context of mobility and injury prevention.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving load tolerance; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

### L03 — Movement modification

- **Outcome:** Apply movement modification in the context of mobility and injury prevention.

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about referral boundaries in the context of mobility and injury prevention.
