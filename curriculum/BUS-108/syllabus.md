## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Automation versus AI

- **Outcome:** Explain automation versus AI in the context of ai and automation.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving automation versus ai; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to ai workflow specification.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain automation versus ai from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of automation versus ai; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BUS-108.L01`
