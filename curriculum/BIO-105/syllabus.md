Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 3; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — DNA variation

- **Outcome:** Explain dNA variation in the context of genetics and disease.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving dna variation; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to fictional genetic report critique.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain dna variation from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of dna variation; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BIO-105.L01`

### L02 — Inheritance

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish inheritance in the context of genetics and disease.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving inheritance; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
