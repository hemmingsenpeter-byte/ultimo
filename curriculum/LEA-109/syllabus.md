## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Governance versus management

- **Outcome:** Explain governance versus management in the context of governance.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving governance versus management; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to fictional board charter.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain governance versus management from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of governance versus management; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `LEA-109.L01`
