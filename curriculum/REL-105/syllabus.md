## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Escalation patterns

- **Outcome:** Explain escalation patterns in the context of conflict repair.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving escalation patterns; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to conflict simulation.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain escalation patterns from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of escalation patterns; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `REL-105.L01`

### L02 — Accountability and apology

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish accountability and apology in the context of conflict repair.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving accountability and apology; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to conflict simulation.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain accountability and apology from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.

- **Competency ID:** `REL-105.L02`

### L03 — Repair and boundaries

- **Outcome:** Apply repair and boundaries in the context of conflict repair.

### L04 — Coercion and safety

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about coercion and safety in the context of conflict repair.
