# COM-101 — Speech and voice

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Voice mechanics

- **Outcome:** Explain voice mechanics in the context of speech and voice.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving voice mechanics; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to recorded explanation or transcript.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain voice mechanics from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of voice mechanics; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `COM-101.L01`

### L02 — Phrasing

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish phrasing in the context of speech and voice.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving phrasing; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to recorded explanation or transcript.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain phrasing from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of phrasing; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `COM-101.L02`

### L03 — Pace and emphasis

- **Outcome:** Apply pace and emphasis in the context of speech and voice.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving pace and emphasis; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to recorded explanation or transcript.

### L04 — Audience adaptation

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about audience adaptation in the context of speech and voice.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving audience adaptation; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to recorded explanation or transcript.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of audience adaptation; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `COM-101.L04`
