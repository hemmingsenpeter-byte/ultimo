## Lesson blueprints and authored materials
### [L01 — Probability and events](L01.md)

- **Outcome:** Explain probability and events in the context of probability and statistics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving probability and events; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to worked probability and survey critique.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain probability and events from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of probability and events; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `INT-103.L01`

## Assessment
Worked probability and survey critique. All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.

**Review:** Education, statistics or philosophy specialist as relevant.. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
