# INT-103 — Probability and statistics

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [INT-101 — Learning how to learn](../INT-101/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Probability and events

- **Outcome:** Explain probability and events in the context of probability and statistics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving probability and events; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to worked probability and survey critique.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain probability and events from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of probability and events; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `INT-103.L01`

### L02 — Descriptive statistics

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish descriptive statistics in the context of probability and statistics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving descriptive statistics; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to worked probability and survey critique.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain descriptive statistics from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of descriptive statistics; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `INT-103.L02`

### L03 — Sampling bias

- **Outcome:** Apply sampling bias in the context of probability and statistics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving sampling bias; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to worked probability and survey critique.

### L04 — Base rates

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about base rates in the context of probability and statistics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving base rates; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of base rates; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `INT-103.L04`
