# INT-103 — Probability and statistics

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours, excluding extended practice. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain probability and events in the context of probability and statistics.
- Compare and distinguish descriptive statistics in the context of probability and statistics.
- Apply sampling bias in the context of probability and statistics.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about base rates in the context of probability and statistics.
