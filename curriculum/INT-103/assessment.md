# INT-103 — Practical assessment

Draft; use private or fictional evidence.

## Capstone

Solve a probability problem, analyse five values including an outlier, critique a fictional survey, and construct a 1,000-item detector frequency table.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Probability and events | Explains the idea accurately | Defines events and assumptions correctly | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L02: Descriptive statistics | Explains the idea accurately | calculates centre and spread | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L03: Sampling bias | Explains the idea accurately | explains selection and nonresponse | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L04: Base rates | Explains the idea accurately | uses correct conditional denominator | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; do not average away missing or safety-critical criteria. Knowledge checks are formative. Level 3 needs delayed independent evidence and appropriate review. Higher levels require repeated application and qualified judgement.

## Feedback

Submit artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next practice action. Retest the unmet criterion. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
