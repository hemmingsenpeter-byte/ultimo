# INT-103 / L02 — Descriptive statistics

## Core teaching

The mean adds values and divides by their count. The median is the middle ordered value (or mean of the two middle values). Range is maximum minus minimum; standard deviation describes dispersion around the mean. Measures answer different questions. The mean can be influenced by extreme values; the median is less sensitive to their magnitude. Always state units and sample definition. A single summary can conceal skew, clusters or unusual cases.

## Framework

Sort → calculate centre → calculate spread → inspect distribution → choose a summary for the decision.

This sequence is an instructional model, not a universal law. Check its limitations against the contrasting case.

## Worked example

Monthly fictional incomes in hundreds: 30,40,50,60,300. Mean=96; median=50; range=270. The unusually large value pulls the mean upward. Neither measure is false; they answer different questions.

### Contrasting case

Reporting only a median hides the upper extreme; reporting only a mean may mislead about a typical person.
