## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Motivation and incentives

- **Outcome:** Explain motivation and incentives in the context of organisational psychology.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving motivation and incentives; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to organisation case analysis.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain motivation and incentives from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of motivation and incentives; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `LEA-105.L01`

### L02 — Role conflict

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish role conflict in the context of organisational psychology.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving role conflict; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
