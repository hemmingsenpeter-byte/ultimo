# CIV-102 — Economics

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 3; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [INT-103 — Probability and statistics](../INT-103/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Scarcity and incentives

- **Outcome:** Explain scarcity and incentives in the context of economics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving scarcity and incentives; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to economic policy brief.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain scarcity and incentives from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of scarcity and incentives; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `CIV-102.L01`

### L02 — Market and nonmarket coordination

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish market and nonmarket coordination in the context of economics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving market and nonmarket coordination; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to economic policy brief.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain market and nonmarket coordination from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of market and nonmarket coordination; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `CIV-102.L02`

### L03 — Macroeconomic indicators

- **Outcome:** Apply macroeconomic indicators in the context of economics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving macroeconomic indicators; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to economic policy brief.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain macroeconomic indicators from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of macroeconomic indicators; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `CIV-102.L03`

### L04 — Distribution

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about distribution in the context of economics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving distribution; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to economic policy brief.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain distribution from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of distribution; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `CIV-102.L04`
