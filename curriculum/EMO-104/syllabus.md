## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Shame and guilt

- **Outcome:** Explain shame and guilt in the context of shame, anger, fear and jealousy.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving shame and guilt; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to scenario response portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain shame and guilt from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of shame and guilt; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `EMO-104.L01`
