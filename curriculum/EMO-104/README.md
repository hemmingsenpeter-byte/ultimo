# EMO-104 — Shame, anger, fear and jealousy

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain shame and guilt in the context of shame, anger, fear and jealousy.
- Compare and distinguish anger fear and jealousy in the context of shame, anger, fear and jealousy.
- Apply needs versus control in the context of shame, anger, fear and jealousy.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about repair in the context of shame, anger, fear and jealousy.
