# EMO-104 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Develop a fictional scenario response portfolio: distinguish shame and guilt, analyse anger/fear/jealousy with evidence limits, separate needs from coercion, and design proportionate repair. Include alternative explanations, a safety exception, observable follow-through and a revision after feedback. No personal disclosure or forced reconciliation.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Shame and guilt | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Distinguishes appraisal scope, establishes responsibility using evidence and proposes proportionate correction and prevention. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Anger fear and jealousy | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Distinguishes anger, fear and jealousy, separates appraisal from evidence and chooses safe, non-coercive actions. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Needs versus control | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Separates needs from preferred solutions and coercive demands, respects duties and identifies feasible alternatives. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Repair | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Proposes evidence-based responsibility, feasible repair and observable prevention without requiring forgiveness or reconciliation. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative only; no clinical or personal emotional assessment.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact against each criterion. Identify one strength, one gap and a corrective task; retest on a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, clinical treatment, distress induction or certification. Independent source, qualified subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
