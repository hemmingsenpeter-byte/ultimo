# COM-105 — Rhetoric and public speaking

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Rhetorical appeals

- **Outcome:** Explain rhetorical appeals in the context of rhetoric and public speaking.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving rhetorical appeals; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to evidence-based speech.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain rhetorical appeals from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of rhetorical appeals; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `COM-105.L01`

### L02 — Speech structure

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish speech structure in the context of rhetoric and public speaking.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving speech structure; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

- **Outcome:** Apply misleading persuasion in the context of rhetoric and public speaking.

### L04 — Delivery

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about delivery in the context of rhetoric and public speaking.
