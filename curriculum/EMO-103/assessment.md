# EMO-103 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Complete a fictional conflict simulation in an ordinary safe setting. Annotate an event, attention, appraisal, response and action tendency; compare three evidence-compatible interpretations including a possible real problem; compare feasible regulation strategies using goals, context and feedback; draft a constructive response and a review step. Provide a contrasting unsafe scenario requiring appropriate support rather than confrontation. Assess reasoning and communication, never calmness, symptom change or disclosure. Written simulation is equally valid.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Triggers and appraisals | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Separates observed events, appraisals, responses and action tendencies; recognises feedback, uncertainty and safety constraints. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Alternative interpretations | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Develops evidence-compatible alternative interpretations and chooses a response that preserves accountability and safety. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Regulation strategies | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Compares regulation options across contexts, selects proportionate strategies and identifies feedback and safety limits. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Constructive responses | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Constructs an evidence-based and respectful response, preserves autonomy and selects an appropriate alternative when direct discussion is unsafe. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no personal traits, emotional states, clinical competence or fitness are assessed.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact against each criterion, identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task, then retest unmet criteria with an equivalent novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No personal testing, diagnosis, treatment, coercion or disclosure. Independent evidence, qualified subject, local safety, cultural and learner review remain required.
