# BUS-102 — Business fundamentals

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 3; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Customer value

- **Outcome:** Explain customer value in the context of business fundamentals.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving customer value; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to business model sheet.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain customer value from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of customer value; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BUS-102.L01`

### L02 — Revenue cost margin
