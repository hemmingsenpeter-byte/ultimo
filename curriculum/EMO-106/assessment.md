# EMO-106 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Prepare a portfolio of fictional boundary scripts: distinguish a self-directed limit, request, agreement, authorised requirement and coercion; draft an optional request and one about an agreed duty; demonstrate a refusal and a counterproposal; design proportionate follow-through and contrast an ordinary safe disagreement with a safety-sensitive scenario requiring appropriate professional, organisational or emergency support. Include accessible alternatives, consent and authority checks, and a revision after delayed feedback. No personal disclosure, clinical evaluation or individual safety plan.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Boundaries versus control | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Distinguishes self-directed limits, requests, agreements, legitimate rules and coercion, with authority and safety checks. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Assertive requests | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Drafts specific, respectful, accessible requests that distinguish optional choices from legitimate obligations and preserve response options. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Declining | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Selects and communicates a proportionate refusal or counterproposal while respecting duties, privacy, consent and safety. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Safety and follow-through | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Chooses safe, proportionate follow-through and distinguishes ordinary disagreement, organisational support, specialist help and emergencies. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no personal traits, emotional states, clinical competence or fitness are assessed.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact against each criterion, identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task, then retest unmet criteria with an equivalent novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No personal testing, diagnosis, treatment, coercion or disclosure. Independent evidence, qualified subject, local safety, cultural and learner review remain required.
