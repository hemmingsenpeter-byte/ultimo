# EMO-106 — Boundaries and assertiveness

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain boundaries versus control in the context of boundaries and assertiveness.
- Compare and distinguish assertive requests in the context of boundaries and assertiveness.
- Apply declining in the context of boundaries and assertiveness.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about safety and follow-through in the context of boundaries and assertiveness.
