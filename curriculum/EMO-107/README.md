# EMO-107 — Grief and vulnerability

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain grief variability in the context of grief and vulnerability.
- Compare and distinguish cultural context in the context of grief and vulnerability.
- Apply voluntary disclosure in the context of grief and vulnerability.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about support options in the context of grief and vulnerability.
