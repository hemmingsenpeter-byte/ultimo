# EMO-107 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Prepare a fictional bereavement support plan: a variable loss/restoration timeline, culturally responsive accommodations, voluntary disclosure and privacy boundaries, practical and qualified support options and emergency routing. Include refusal, accessible alternatives and a flexible review point. No personal loss narrative, fixed grief stages or diagnosis.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Grief variability | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Explains variability and distinguishes model-based description from prescribed stages or clinical assessment. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Cultural context | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Compares cultural and individual preferences and proposes accessible, consent-based accommodations with appropriate limits. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Voluntary disclosure | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Demonstrates permission-based communication, privacy and refusal boundaries, and appropriate safety exceptions. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Support options | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Matches practical, cultural, qualified and emergency support routes to needs while respecting choice and role limits. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away omissions or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no personal emotional or clinical assessment.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest unmet criteria on a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, clinical treatment, distress induction or certification. Independent evidence, qualified subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
