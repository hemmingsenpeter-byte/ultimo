# BIO-107 — Sexual and reproductive health

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Reproductive anatomy

- **Outcome:** Explain reproductive anatomy in the context of sexual and reproductive health.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving reproductive anatomy; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to sexual health resource comparison.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain reproductive anatomy from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of reproductive anatomy; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BIO-107.L01`

### L02 — Prevention and testing

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish prevention and testing in the context of sexual and reproductive health.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving prevention and testing; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to sexual health resource comparison.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain prevention and testing from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of prevention and testing; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BIO-107.L02`

### L03 — Fertility literacy

- **Outcome:** Apply fertility literacy in the context of sexual and reproductive health.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving fertility literacy; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to sexual health resource comparison.

### L04 — Consent and referral

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about consent and referral in the context of sexual and reproductive health.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving consent and referral; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to sexual health resource comparison.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of consent and referral; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BIO-107.L04`
