# BUS-105 — Marketing

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 3; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Audience and positioning

- **Outcome:** Explain audience and positioning in the context of marketing.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving audience and positioning; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to campaign test brief.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain audience and positioning from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of audience and positioning; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BUS-105.L01`

### L02 — Acquisition channels
