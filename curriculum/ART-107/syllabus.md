# ART-107 — Dance

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Rhythm and space

- **Outcome:** Explain rhythm and space in the context of dance.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving rhythm and space; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to choreography study.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain rhythm and space from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of rhythm and space; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-107.L01`

### L02 — Dance traditions

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish dance traditions in the context of dance.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving dance traditions; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to choreography study.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain dance traditions from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of dance traditions; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-107.L02`

### L03 — Accessible movement

- **Outcome:** Apply accessible movement in the context of dance.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving accessible movement; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to choreography study.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain accessible movement from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of accessible movement; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-107.L03`

### L04 — Expression and consent

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about expression and consent in the context of dance.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving expression and consent; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to choreography study.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain expression and consent from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of expression and consent; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-107.L04`
