# MEN-104 — Compulsive behaviour

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain compulsion and impairment in the context of compulsive behaviour.
- Compare and distinguish contextual triggers in the context of compulsive behaviour.
- Apply support pathways in the context of compulsive behaviour.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about withdrawal and referral boundaries in the context of compulsive behaviour.
