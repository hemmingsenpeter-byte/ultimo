# MEN-104 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Use fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Prepare a fictional support map comparing a normal routine, a reported impairing pattern and a withdrawal-related concern. Distinguish behaviour, context, control and impact; map potential influences without inducing or testing urges; identify consent-based support routes, accessibility alternatives and emergency exceptions; explain why clinical diagnosis, exposure therapy and substance or medication discontinuation advice require appropriate qualified care. Do not use personal histories, screening scores, taper schedules or self-experiments.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Compulsion and impairment | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Distinguishes routine, possible compulsion, reported impairment and physical dependence without diagnosing; identifies appropriate support. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Contextual triggers | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Maps contextual influences, distinguishes hypotheses from causes and proposes voluntary support without inducing distress. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Support pathways | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Constructs a feasible, consent-based support pathway with alternatives, access considerations and emergency boundaries. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Withdrawal and referral boundaries | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Recognises withdrawal-related referral boundaries and selects medical or emergency help without giving a discontinuation plan. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must be demonstrated at Level 2. No averaging away omissions or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz results are formative only; no personal health or clinical competency is assessed.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact against the criteria; identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest with an equivalent novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives are acceptable. No private disclosure, diagnosis, treatment, distress induction or unqualified certification. Independent subject, safety, academic and learner review required.
