# MEN-104 — Compulsive behaviour

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Compulsion and impairment

- **Outcome:** Explain compulsion and impairment in the context of compulsive behaviour.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving compulsion and impairment; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to fictional support map.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain compulsion and impairment from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of compulsion and impairment; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `MEN-104.L01`

### L02 — Contextual triggers

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish contextual triggers in the context of compulsive behaviour.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving contextual triggers; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
