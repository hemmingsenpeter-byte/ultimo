# BUS-101 — Career design

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [INT-101 — Learning how to learn](../INT-101/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Skills and constraints

- **Outcome:** Explain skills and constraints in the context of career design.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving skills and constraints; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to career options portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain skills and constraints from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of skills and constraints; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BUS-101.L01`

### L02 — Role research

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish role research in the context of career design.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving role research; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to career options portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain role research from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of role research; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BUS-101.L02`

### L03 — Career experiments

- **Outcome:** Apply career experiments in the context of career design.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving career experiments; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to career options portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain career experiments from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of career experiments; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BUS-101.L03`

### L04 — Development plan

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about development plan in the context of career design.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving development plan; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to career options portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain development plan from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of development plan; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BUS-101.L04`

## Assessment
**Artifact:** Career options portfolio.

All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.

**Review:** Practitioner with relevant business, technology or privacy expertise.. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
