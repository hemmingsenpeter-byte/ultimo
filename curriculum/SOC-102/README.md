# SOC-102 — Social awareness

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain ambiguous cues in the context of social awareness.
- Compare and distinguish culture and context in the context of social awareness.
- Apply group norms in the context of social awareness.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about clarifying questions in the context of social awareness.

## Reading
- [Egocentrism over e-mail: can we communicate as well as we think?](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16393025/) — 2005, abstract and experimental studies on communicating tone.
- [Perspective taking as egocentric anchoring and adjustment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15382983/) — 2004, abstract and perspective-taking experiments.
- [Measuring intercultural dialogue: a conceptual and technical framework](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/measuring-intercultural-dialogue-conceptual-and-technical-framework) — 2020, overview of dialogue and cultural diversity.
- [A Focus Theory of Normative Conduct: A Theoretical Refinement and Reevaluation of the Role of Norms in Human Behavior](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0065260108603305) — 1991, abstract, descriptive and injunctive norms.
- [Enhancing Motivation for Change in Substance Use Disorder Treatment, TIP 35, Chapter 3: Motivational Interviewing as a Counseling Style](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK571068/) — 2019, reflective listening section.
