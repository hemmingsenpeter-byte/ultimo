# SOC-102 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Complete a fictional social scenario analysis with four cases: ambiguous cues, cultural/contextual variation, conflicting descriptive and injunctive norms, and a clarification decision. Separate observation from inference, identify alternatives, check authority and accessibility, include a case where no question is needed and revise a decision after feedback. No real-person profiling or surveillance.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Ambiguous cues | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Separates cues from inference, generates alternatives and selects proportionate clarification or action. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Culture and context | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Compares contextual explanations and adapts communication respectfully without demographic stereotyping. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Group norms | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Distinguishes observed practice, approval and authorised rules, and selects safe, fair responses to conflicting norms. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Clarifying questions | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Writes proportionate, neutral questions and updates decisions while respecting privacy, autonomy and safety. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent performance and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no assessment of personal traits, popularity or clinical competence.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and one corrective task; retest unmet criteria using a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, real-person profiling, coercion, clinical treatment or certification. Independent evidence, qualified subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
