# SOC-102 — Social awareness

Framework defined; lesson content: **draft**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Ambiguous cues

- **Outcome:** Explain ambiguous cues in the context of social awareness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving ambiguous cues; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to social scenario analysis.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain ambiguous cues from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of ambiguous cues; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `SOC-102.L01`

### L02 — Culture and context

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish culture and context in the context of social awareness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving culture and context; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to social scenario analysis.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain culture and context from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of culture and context; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `SOC-102.L02`

### L03 — Group norms

- **Outcome:** Apply group norms in the context of social awareness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving group norms; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

### L04 — Clarifying questions

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about clarifying questions in the context of social awareness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving clarifying questions; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of clarifying questions; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `SOC-102.L04`
