# CIV-106 — Energy and infrastructure

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 3; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Energy forms

- **Outcome:** Explain energy forms in the context of energy and infrastructure.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving energy forms; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to infrastructure systems model.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain energy forms from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of energy forms; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `CIV-106.L01`

### L02 — Infrastructure networks
