# MEN-102 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Use fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Submit a fictional attention audit for a study or office task. Map selective, sustained and working-memory demands; analyse an interruption log with urgent exceptions and resumption cues; design two protected-practice variants with comparable quality criteria; interpret a synthetic recovery case and identify when rest, workload changes or professional help are appropriate. Include a clear comparison, assumptions, one counterexample and a revised plan. No intentional fatigue, sleep loss or clinical self-testing.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Attention limits | Accurately defines concepts and distinguishes the counterexample | Distinguishes three attentional demands, identifies a task-specific support and interprets a comparison without diagnosing ability. | Applies the criterion to an unfamiliar fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L02: Interruptions | Accurately defines concepts and distinguishes the counterexample | Classifies interruptions proportionately, preserves urgent obligations and demonstrates reliable resumption. | Applies the criterion to an unfamiliar fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L03: Protected practice | Accurately defines concepts and distinguishes the counterexample | Designs accessible, bounded practice with a measurable deliverable, resumption and safety exceptions. | Applies the criterion to an unfamiliar fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L04: Rest | Accurately defines concepts and distinguishes the counterexample | Justifies a recovery choice using quality and safety, recognises competing explanations and appropriate referral. | Applies the criterion to an unfamiliar fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises after delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2. No averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent performance and appropriate reviewer judgement; quiz scores are formative. Evidence does not establish mental health status, fitness or clinical competency.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact for one strength, one gap and one concrete corrective action. Retest unmet criteria with a new case. Written, oral, visual or simulated equivalents are acceptable. No diagnosis, treatment, private disclosure, unsafe practice or automatic mastery award. Refer clinical concerns to appropriately qualified professionals. Independent source, qualified subject and learner review required.
