# MEN-102 — Attention and concentration

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain attention limits in the context of attention and concentration.
- Compare and distinguish interruptions in the context of attention and concentration.
- Apply protected practice in the context of attention and concentration.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about rest in the context of attention and concentration.

## Reading
- [The magical number 4 in short-term memory: a reconsideration of mental storage capacity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11515286/) — 2001, abstract, conditions for capacity estimates.
- [Executive control of cognitive processes in task switching](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11518143/) — 2001, abstract, rule complexity and task cues.
- [Why is it so hard to do my work? The challenge of attention residue when switching between work tasks](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2009.04.002) — 2009, abstract, transitions between work tasks.
- [Implementation intentions and goal achievement: a meta-analysis of effects and processes](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601%2806%2938002-1) — 2006, abstract and review of situation-response plans.
- [Give me a break! A systematic review and meta-analysis on the efficacy of micro-breaks for increasing well-being and performance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36044424/) — 2022, abstract and pooled results.
- [Sleep deprivation and deficiency: How sleep affects your health](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation/health-effects) — 2022, daytime functioning and health effects.
