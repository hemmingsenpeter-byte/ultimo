# INT-108 — Practical assessment

Draft; fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Prepare a forecast journal with independently resolvable fictional questions, two defensible reference classes, a decision payoff table and a ten-event synthetic scoring dataset. Compute expected value and Brier score by hand or spreadsheet, compare a baseline, and identify uncertainty and hard constraints.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Resolvable forecasts | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Writes an independently resolvable question and tests boundary cases. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L02: Reference classes | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Selects defensible reference classes and explains adjustments and sampling limitations. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L03: Expected value | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Builds correct payoff tables and compares sensitivity and downside constraints. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L04: Calibration | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Computes a Brier score, interprets calibration cautiously and compares a fixed baseline. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quizzes are formative; self-recorded work is not certification.

## Feedback

Provide a fictional artifact, reasoning, assumptions and corrections. Reviewer identifies one strength, one gap and a specific next task. Retest unmet criteria. Equivalent accessible written, spoken, diagrammatic or simulated evidence is accepted. Independent source, subject and learner review required.
