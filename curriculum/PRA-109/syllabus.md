# PRA-109 — Emergency preparedness

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Local hazards and alerts

- **Outcome:** Explain local hazards and alerts in the context of emergency preparedness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving local hazards and alerts; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to household emergency plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain local hazards and alerts from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of local hazards and alerts; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-109.L01`

### L02 — Supplies and communication

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish supplies and communication in the context of emergency preparedness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving supplies and communication; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to household emergency plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain supplies and communication from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of supplies and communication; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-109.L02`

### L03 — Evacuation and shelter

- **Outcome:** Apply evacuation and shelter in the context of emergency preparedness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving evacuation and shelter; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to household emergency plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain evacuation and shelter from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.

### L04 — Accessibility

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about accessibility in the context of emergency preparedness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving accessibility; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to household emergency plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain accessibility from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of accessibility; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-109.L04`
