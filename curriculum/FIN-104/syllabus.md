## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Present bias

- **Outcome:** Explain present bias in the context of financial psychology.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving present bias; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to financial decision journal.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain present bias from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of present bias; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `FIN-104.L01`

### L02 — Social pressure

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish social pressure in the context of financial psychology.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving social pressure; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
