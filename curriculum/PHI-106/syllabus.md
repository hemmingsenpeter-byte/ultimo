# PHI-106 — Existentialism

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 5; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [PHI-105 — Ethics](../PHI-105/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Freedom and responsibility

- **Outcome:** Explain freedom and responsibility in the context of existentialism.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving freedom and responsibility; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to existential dilemma essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain freedom and responsibility from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of freedom and responsibility; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-106.L01`

### L02 — Existentialist thinkers

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish existentialist thinkers in the context of existentialism.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving existentialist thinkers; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to existential dilemma essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain existentialist thinkers from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of existentialist thinkers; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-106.L02`

### L03 — Authenticity and constraints

- **Outcome:** Apply authenticity and constraints in the context of existentialism.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving authenticity and constraints; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

### L04 — Objections

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about objections in the context of existentialism.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of objections; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-106.L04`
