# SOC-108 — Practical assessment

Draft — independent review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Develop an ethical networking plan using synthetic contacts. Map relationships and information bridges, draft three transparent bounded requests, design a double opt-in introduction and minimal follow-up log. Include refusal, unanswered outreach, actual commitments, privacy boundaries and an independent public alternative. Evaluate accuracy, feasibility and consent rather than response rates or contact count.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Reciprocal networks | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Maps reciprocal relationships and possible information bridges while bounding research claims and respecting autonomy. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Low-pressure requests | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Writes bounded, transparent, proportionate requests with meaningful refusal and appropriate information boundaries. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Consent-based introductions | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Designs permission-based introductions with clear purpose, minimum information, realistic expectations and refusal handling. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Follow-up | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Designs reliable, proportionate follow-up that fulfils agreements, respects refusal and minimises information collection. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no popularity, appearance or personality assessment.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest unmet criteria on a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No private disclosure, coercion, unwanted contact or certification. Independent evidence, subject, cultural, safety, accessibility and learner review required.
