# SOC-108 — Networking

Draft · Year 3 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain reciprocal networks in the context of networking.
- Compare and distinguish low-pressure requests in the context of networking.
- Apply consent-based introductions in the context of networking.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about follow-up in the context of networking.

## Reading
- [The Strength of Weak Ties](https://sociology.stanford.edu/publications/strength-weak-ties) — 1973, network theory and bridging ties.
- [A causal test of the strength of weak ties](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36107999/) — 2022, abstract, LinkedIn experiments and job mobility.
- [Improving Assertiveness Self-Help Resources](https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/Resources/Looking-After-Yourself/Assertiveness) — Workbook overview, assertiveness, requests and declining.
- [How many hours does it take to make a friend?](https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407518761225) — 2019 issue; first published 2018, studies of time and friendship closeness.
- [Principle 11 — Disclosure of personal information](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/11/) — Public explanation of limits and grounds for disclosure.
