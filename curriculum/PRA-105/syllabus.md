# PRA-105 — Driving and mechanics

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Road risk

- **Outcome:** Explain road risk in the context of driving and mechanics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving road risk; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to vehicle and journey checklist.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain road risk from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of road risk; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-105.L01`

### L02 — Vehicle systems

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish vehicle systems in the context of driving and mechanics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving vehicle systems; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to vehicle and journey checklist.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain vehicle systems from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of vehicle systems; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-105.L02`

### L03 — Inspection and warning signs

- **Outcome:** Apply inspection and warning signs in the context of driving and mechanics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving inspection and warning signs; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to vehicle and journey checklist.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain inspection and warning signs from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of inspection and warning signs; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-105.L03`

### L04 — Local rules

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about local rules in the context of driving and mechanics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving local rules; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Analyse an unfamiliar case; defend a choice, identify uncertainty and revise after feedback. Connect the result to vehicle and journey checklist.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain local rules from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of local rules; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PRA-105.L04`

**Artifact:** Vehicle and journey checklist.

All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.
