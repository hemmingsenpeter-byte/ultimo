# PHY-103 — Cardiovascular conditioning

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Energy systems

- **Outcome:** Explain energy systems in the context of cardiovascular conditioning.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving energy systems; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to conditioning plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain energy systems from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of energy systems; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-103.L01`

### L02 — Intensity measures

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish intensity measures in the context of cardiovascular conditioning.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving intensity measures; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

### L03 — Gradual conditioning
