# Feedback and transfer

**Objective:** Use corrective feedback and test transfer.

Feedback identifies discrepancies between intended and actual performance. Effective practice requires a specific target, a meaningful attempt, information about mistakes and a subsequent attempt. Transfer means using what you learned in a different context; it is not automatic.

Example: after learning to distinguish correlation from causation, evaluate a new claim about sleep and productivity. Identify a possible confounder and describe evidence that would strengthen the causal claim.

**Knowledge check:** Why is repeating the identical question insufficient evidence of transfer?

**Practice:** Ask a peer to review your explanation against a short rubric, or compare it against a credible source. Create a novel case and solve it. Revise your learning plan based on the results.
