# Retrieval and spaced practice

**Objective:** Design retrieval sessions distributed over time.

Retrieval practice means attempting to recall information before consulting notes. Spacing means distributing study over time rather than concentrating it into one session. The research reviews in the reading list support practice testing and distributed practice; the appropriate interval varies with material and retention goals.

Example: after reading about a concept, close the book and reconstruct its explanation, then correct your answer. Repeat after a day, three days and seven days as an illustrative schedule, not an optimal universal prescription.

**Knowledge check:** Compare two equal-time plans: rereading four times today or retrieving across four days with correction. Which is more likely to support delayed recall, and what limits your prediction?

**Practice:** Write five questions that require recall or application. Test yourself before looking at the answers. Record accuracy and correct mistakes. Schedule the next review.
