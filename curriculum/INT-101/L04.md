# Build your own learning system

**Objective:** Execute and evaluate a practical learning cycle.

A sustainable learning system begins with a meaningful goal, a baseline, a short practice routine, feedback and scheduled review. Protect rest and choose a workload you can maintain. A learning log is a tool, not a measure of worth.

**Worked example:** Goal: explain compound interest and compare two savings scenarios. Baseline: cannot explain the difference between simple and compound interest. Practice: three short study sessions, retrieval questions, a worked example and a new problem. Evaluation: delayed closed-book explanation and calculation, followed by correction.

**Capstone:** Choose one useful topic. Run a seven-day cycle with at least three practice sessions and a delayed assessment. Record baseline, practice, feedback, a novel application, and a revised plan. Use the rubric in assessment.md. Repeat on a different topic before claiming consistent independent competence.
