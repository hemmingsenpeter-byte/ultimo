# ART-103 — Literature and poetry

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Imagery and voice

- **Outcome:** Explain imagery and voice in the context of literature and poetry.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving imagery and voice; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to essay and original poem.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain imagery and voice from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of imagery and voice; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `ART-103.L01`

### L02 — Genre

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish genre in the context of literature and poetry.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving genre; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

### L03 — Competing interpretations

- **Outcome:** Apply competing interpretations in the context of literature and poetry.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of close reading; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
