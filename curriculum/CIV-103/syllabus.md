# CIV-103 — Law and rights

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Law ethics rights

- **Outcome:** Explain law ethics rights in the context of law and rights.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving law ethics rights; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to legal literacy case.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain law ethics rights from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of law ethics rights; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `CIV-103.L01`

### L02 — Jurisdictions and institutions
