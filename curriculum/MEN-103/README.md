# MEN-103 — Motivation and procrastination

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain avoidance in the context of motivation and procrastination.
- Compare and distinguish task decomposition in the context of motivation and procrastination.
- Apply if-then plans in the context of motivation and procrastination.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about barrier review in the context of motivation and procrastination.

## Reading
- [The nature of procrastination: a meta-analytic and theoretical review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17201571/) — 2007, abstract, predictors and temporal motivation theory.
- [Procrastination and the Priority of Short-Term Mood Regulation](https://doi.org/10.1111/spc3.12011) — 2013, abstract, short-term mood repair account.
- [Targeting Procrastination Using Psychological Treatments: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6125391/) — 2018, intervention literature and limitations.
- [Implementation intentions and goal achievement: a meta-analysis of effects and processes](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601%2806%2938002-1) — 2006, abstract and review of situation-response plans.
- [Stress](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/stress) — Web Q&A, stress, daily functioning and seeking help; accessed 2026-11-26.
