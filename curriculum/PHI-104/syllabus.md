# PHI-104 — Comparative religion

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 4; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [INT-102 — Logic and argument](../INT-102/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Descriptive and normative claims

- **Outcome:** Explain descriptive and normative claims in the context of comparative religion.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving descriptive and normative claims; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to religion case analysis.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain descriptive and normative claims from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of descriptive and normative claims; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-104.L01`

### L02 — Traditions and diversity

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish traditions and diversity in the context of comparative religion.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving traditions and diversity; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to religion case analysis.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain traditions and diversity from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of traditions and diversity; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-104.L02`

### L03 — Source evaluation

- **Outcome:** Apply source evaluation in the context of comparative religion.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving source evaluation; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

### L04 — Coexistence

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about coexistence in the context of comparative religion.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of coexistence; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-104.L04`

## Assessment
**Artifact:** Religion case analysis.

All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.

**Review:** Philosopher or scholar of relevant tradition; avoid treating one tradition as universal.. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
