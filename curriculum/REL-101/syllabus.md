## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Attraction and autonomy

- **Outcome:** Explain attraction and autonomy in the context of attraction and dating.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving attraction and autonomy; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to dating scenario analysis.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain attraction and autonomy from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of attraction and autonomy; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `REL-101.L01`
