# EMO-105 — Attachment and development

Framework defined; lesson content: **draft**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Attachment research

- **Outcome:** Explain attachment research in the context of attachment and development.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving attachment research; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to attachment case critique.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain attachment research from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of attachment research; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `EMO-105.L01`

### L02 — Development

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish development in the context of attachment and development.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving development; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
