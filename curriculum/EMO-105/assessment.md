# EMO-105 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Write a fictional attachment case critique: explain safe haven and secure base, distinguish research methods, interpret the caregiver-sensitivity association, map development with competing hypotheses, critique circular labels and draft bounded responsive communication. Include limitations and a revised response after feedback. Do not test or classify real people.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Attachment research | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Explains attachment functions and research methods and limits inference from ordinary observations. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Development | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Explains developmental influences and interprets group-level evidence without deterministic causation or caregiver blame. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Label limitations | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Critiques circular label-based explanations, limits measurement claims and addresses actual conduct without diagnosis. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Responsive communication | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Designs responsive, bounded communication that checks the need, respects autonomy and avoids unsupported attachment labels. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative only; no clinical or personal emotional assessment.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact against each criterion. Identify one strength, one gap and a corrective task; retest on a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, clinical treatment, distress induction or certification. Independent source, qualified subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
