# FIN-109 — Succession

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 5; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Ownership and beneficiaries

- **Outcome:** Explain ownership and beneficiaries in the context of succession.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving ownership and beneficiaries; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to succession checklist.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain ownership and beneficiaries from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of ownership and beneficiaries; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `FIN-109.L01`

### L02 — Continuity risks

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish continuity risks in the context of succession.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving continuity risks; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to succession checklist.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain continuity risks from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of continuity risks; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `FIN-109.L02`

### L03 — Governance

- **Outcome:** Apply governance in the context of succession.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving governance; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to succession checklist.

- **Competency ID:** `FIN-109.L03`

### L04 — Professional advice

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about professional advice in the context of succession.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving professional advice; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of professional advice; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `FIN-109.L04`
