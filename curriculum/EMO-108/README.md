# EMO-108 — Empathy

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain perspective-taking in the context of empathy.
- Compare and distinguish reflective listening in the context of empathy.
- Apply empathy versus agreement in the context of empathy.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about compassionate limits in the context of empathy.
