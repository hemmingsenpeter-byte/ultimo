# EMO-108 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Produce a fictional listening role-play or written equivalent. Map two viewpoints, reflect content and meaning, incorporate a correction, acknowledge an experience while disagreeing with a factual claim, and communicate a compassionate limit and alternate route. Retest a novel case with feedback; assess communication not emotions.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Perspective-taking | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Builds a tentative, evidence-based perspective, considers alternatives and respects autonomy and uncertainty. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Reflective listening | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Produces accurate reflections, responds to correction, offers relevant assistance and maintains non-clinical role boundaries. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Empathy versus agreement | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Acknowledges experience without endorsing unsupported claims or coercive requests, maintaining evidence and accountability. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Compassionate limits | Defines concepts and explains a contrasting case | Selects feasible, compassionate assistance with consent, role limits, alternative routes and safety boundaries. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away omissions or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no personal emotional or clinical assessment.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest unmet criteria on a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, clinical treatment, distress induction or certification. Independent evidence, qualified subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
