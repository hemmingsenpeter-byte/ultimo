# FIN-101 — Budget and balance sheet

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours, excluding extended practice. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain financial stocks and flows in the context of budget and balance sheet.

- Apply irregular expenses in the context of budget and balance sheet.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about stress testing in the context of budget and balance sheet.
