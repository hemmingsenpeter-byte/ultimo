# FIN-101 — Practical assessment

Draft; use private or fictional evidence.

## Capstone

Prepare a fictional NZD balance sheet and monthly budget including irregular costs, two stress scenarios and review triggers.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Financial stocks and flows | Explains the idea accurately | Distinguishes stocks and flows with correct net worth | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L02: Budget and balance sheet | Explains the idea accurately | computes monthly surplus | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L03: Irregular expenses | Explains the idea accurately | converts annual costs without double counting | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L04: Stress testing | Explains the idea accurately | recalculates downside and identifies limits and support | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; do not average away missing or safety-critical criteria. Knowledge checks are formative. Level 3 needs delayed independent evidence and appropriate review. Higher levels require repeated application and qualified judgement.

## Feedback

Submit artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next practice action. Retest the unmet criterion. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required. Draft educational framework only. Use fictional cases; no personal diagnosis, prescription, hazardous practice, financial/legal instruction or unqualified certification. Obtain qualified/local review before publication.
