# MEN-101 — Habits

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours, excluding extended practice. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain cues and routines in the context of habits.
- Compare and distinguish environmental friction in the context of habits.
- Apply implementation intentions in the context of habits.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about iteration in the context of habits.
