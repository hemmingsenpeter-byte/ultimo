## Lesson blueprints and authored materials
### [L01 — Cues and routines](L01.md)

- **Outcome:** Explain cues and routines in the context of habits.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving cues and routines; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to seven-day habit experiment.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain cues and routines from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of cues and routines; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `MEN-101.L01`

## Assessment
Seven-day habit experiment. All mapped criteria at level 2; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires independent novel application, delayed reassessment and appropriate review. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required.

**Review:** mental-health reviewer where applicable. Draft educational framework only. Use fictional cases; no personal diagnosis, prescription, hazardous practice, financial/legal instruction or unqualified certification. Obtain qualified/local review before publication.
