# MEN-105 — Resilience

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Resilience and resources

- **Outcome:** Explain resilience and resources in the context of resilience.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving resilience and resources; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to fictional recovery plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain resilience and resources from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of resilience and resources; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `MEN-105.L01`

### L02 — Actionable stressors

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish actionable stressors in the context of resilience.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving actionable stressors; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
