# MEN-105 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Use fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Develop a fictional recovery plan for a person facing a disrupted routine and resource gap. Map adversity and resources across individual, social and institutional levels; separate direct action, influence and uncertainty; draft bounded support requests and alternatives; design a flexible review plan with two possible trajectories. Include structural change, rest and professional referral where appropriate. No trauma disclosure, personal resilience grading or medical readiness assessment.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Resilience and resources | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Describes adaptation in context, distinguishes resources from outcomes and identifies structural as well as personal support. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Actionable stressors | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Distinguishes controllable actions from uncertain outcomes, compares feasible responses and identifies authority and safety limits. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Social support | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Matches support types to needs and drafts consent-based requests, alternatives and role boundaries. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Recovery | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Designs a flexible recovery plan, recognises resource changes and distinguishes educational planning from clinical clearance. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must be demonstrated at Level 2. No averaging away omissions or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz results are formative only; no personal health or clinical competency is assessed.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact against the criteria; identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest with an equivalent novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives are acceptable. No private disclosure, diagnosis, treatment, distress induction or unqualified certification. Independent subject, safety, academic and learner review required.
