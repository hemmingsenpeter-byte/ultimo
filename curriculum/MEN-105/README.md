# MEN-105 — Resilience

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain resilience and resources in the context of resilience.
- Compare and distinguish actionable stressors in the context of resilience.
- Apply social support in the context of resilience.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about recovery in the context of resilience.

## Reading
- [Resilience definitions, theory, and challenges: interdisciplinary perspectives](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25317257/) — 2014, abstract and interdisciplinary definitions.
- [Resilience in the Context of Chronic Stress and Health in Adults](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4494753/) — 2011 review, taxonomy of resilience resources.
- [Mental health at work](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-health-at-work) — Fact sheet, psychosocial risks and organisational interventions; accessed 2026-11-26.
- [Stress, social support, and the buffering hypothesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3901065/) — 1985, abstract, main-effect and buffering models.
- [The Recovery Experience Questionnaire](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17638488/) — 2007, abstract, four recovery experience dimensions.
