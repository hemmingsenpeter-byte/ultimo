# EMO-102 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Write a personality-test critique for a fictional tool claiming to predict leadership or relationship compatibility. Explain trait dimensions and facets versus states and temperament; analyse a synthetic repeated-observation dataset with context; distinguish reliability, validity, norms and intended use; critique cultural comparability and provide a fair alternative evidence route. Include at least one counterexample, three missing evidence items and a revised recommendation after feedback. Do not administer a test, collect personal responses, diagnose or rank cultural groups.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Trait models | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Explains trait dimensions and facets, differentiates temperament, states and skills, and limits inference from fictional observations. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Situational variation | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Compares fictional behavioural distributions and contextual conditions, calculates descriptive summaries and avoids unsupported stable-trait claims. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Measurement | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Audits a fictional instrument's construct, consistency, validity, norms and intended use without administering or diagnosing. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Cultural context | Defines key concepts and explains the contrasting case | Critiques cultural comparability, avoids stereotyping and recommends a justified alternative or qualified review. | Applies the criterion independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no personal traits, emotional states, clinical competence or fitness are assessed.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact against each criterion, identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task, then retest unmet criteria with an equivalent novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No personal testing, diagnosis, treatment, coercion or disclosure. Independent evidence, qualified subject, local safety, cultural and learner review remain required.
