# EMO-102 — Personality and temperament

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain trait models in the context of personality and temperament.
- Compare and distinguish situational variation in the context of personality and temperament.
- Apply measurement in the context of personality and temperament.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about cultural context in the context of personality and temperament.
