## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Trait models

- **Outcome:** Explain trait models in the context of personality and temperament.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving trait models; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to personality-test critique.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain trait models from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of trait models; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `EMO-102.L01`

### L02 — Situational variation
