# INT-105 — Practical assessment

Draft; fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Audit a fictional committee's preference for one of two service providers. Produce a symmetric evidence table, an independent estimate and denominator check, a scoped counterexample, and a two-pass decision journal. Explain a revision condition, limitations and how you would avoid evaluating the process solely by its outcome.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Confirmation bias | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Identifies selective handling of evidence and designs a symmetric test. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L02: Anchoring and availability | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Separates both mechanisms and uses appropriate independent evidence. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L03: Counterexamples | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Constructs in-scope challenges and distinguishes universal from statistical claims. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L04: Decision journals | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Records prior reasoning and separates outcome, luck and process during review. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quizzes are formative; self-recorded work is not certification.

## Feedback

Provide a fictional artifact, reasoning, assumptions and corrections. Reviewer identifies one strength, one gap and a specific next task. Retest unmet criteria. Equivalent accessible written, spoken, diagrammatic or simulated evidence is accepted. Independent source, subject and learner review required.
