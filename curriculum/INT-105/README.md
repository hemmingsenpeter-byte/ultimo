# INT-105 — Cognitive biases

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain confirmation bias in the context of cognitive biases.
- Compare and distinguish anchoring and availability in the context of cognitive biases.
- Apply counterexamples in the context of cognitive biases.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about decision journals in the context of cognitive biases.

## Reading
- [Confirmation Bias: A Ubiquitous Phenomenon in Many Guises](https://doi.org/10.1037/1089-2680.2.2.175) — 1998, abstract and review topic.
- [Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.185.4157.1124) — 1974, abstract, availability and anchoring.
- [Logical Consequence](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2014/entries/logical-consequence/) — Model-centred approach to validity and counterexamples.
- [Outcome bias in decision evaluation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3367280/) — 1988, abstract and outcome-bias experiments.
