# COM-103 — Practical assessment

Draft; use private or fictional evidence.

## Capstone

Write or role-play a fictional listening simulation with correction, open/closed questions, fair summary and optional sensitive question with refusal.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Reflective listening | Explains the idea accurately | Verifies paraphrase | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L02: Question types | Explains the idea accurately | chooses question types without leading assumptions | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L03: Fair summary | Explains the idea accurately | represents viewpoints fairly | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L04: Consent-based questioning | Explains the idea accurately | respects refusal and privacy | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; do not average away missing or safety-critical criteria. Knowledge checks are formative. Level 3 needs delayed independent evidence and appropriate review. Higher levels require repeated application and qualified judgement.

## Feedback

Submit artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next practice action. Retest the unmet criterion. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required. Respect consent, privacy and applicable rules; source and subject review before publication.
