# MEN-107 — Performance under pressure

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 3; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Pressure and attention

- **Outcome:** Explain pressure and attention in the context of performance under pressure.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving pressure and attention; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to presentation rehearsal.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain pressure and attention from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of pressure and attention; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `MEN-107.L01`

### L02 — Low-stakes rehearsal

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish low-stakes rehearsal in the context of performance under pressure.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving low-stakes rehearsal; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
