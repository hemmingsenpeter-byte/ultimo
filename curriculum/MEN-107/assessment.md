# MEN-107 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Use fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Create an accessible, voluntary presentation rehearsal using fictional information. Define criteria and plausible attention demands; complete two equivalent low-stakes practice rounds or analyse synthetic attempts; separate process and outcome evidence; write a structured debrief and revise the next attempt. Provide an alternative written format and stop rule. No deliberate distress induction, punitive testing or clinical assessment. Demonstrate transfer using a new case.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Pressure and attention | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Distinguishes pressure mechanisms, proposes rival explanations and chooses safe task-specific support. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Low-stakes rehearsal | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Designs voluntary accessible rehearsal with specific objectives, feedback, safe variation and transfer limits. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Process and outcomes | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Separates observable process quality from outcome, recognises non-controllable factors and selects an ethical improvement. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Debrief | Defines the central concepts and explains a contrasting example | Conducts an evidence-based, respectful debrief that identifies a testable improvement without making a personal or clinical judgement. | Applies the criterion to a novel fictional case without prompts, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must be demonstrated at Level 2. No averaging away omissions or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz results are formative only; no personal health or clinical competency is assessed.

## Feedback and boundaries

Review the fictional artefact against the criteria; identify one strength, one gap and a specific corrective task. Retest with an equivalent novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives are acceptable. No private disclosure, diagnosis, treatment, distress induction or unqualified certification. Independent subject, safety, academic and learner review required.
