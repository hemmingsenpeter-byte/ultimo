# MEN-107 — Performance under pressure

Draft · Year 3 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain pressure and attention in the context of performance under pressure.
- Compare and distinguish low-stakes rehearsal in the context of performance under pressure.
- Apply process and outcomes in the context of performance under pressure.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about debrief in the context of performance under pressure.

- [Do team and individual debriefs enhance performance? A meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23516804/) — 2013, abstract, structured debriefs and performance.
- [A meta-analysis of the effectiveness of the after-action review (or debrief) and factors that influence its effectiveness](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32852990/) — 2021, abstract, after-action review and moderators.
