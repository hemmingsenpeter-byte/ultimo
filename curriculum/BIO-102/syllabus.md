# BIO-102 — Nutrition and metabolism

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [BIO-101 — Human biology](../BIO-101/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Nutrients

- **Outcome:** Explain nutrients in the context of nutrition and metabolism.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving nutrients; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to fictional meal audit.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain nutrients from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of nutrients; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `BIO-102.L01`

### L02 — Energy balance

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish energy balance in the context of nutrition and metabolism.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving energy balance; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to fictional meal audit.

### L03 — Dietary patterns

- **Outcome:** Apply dietary patterns in the context of nutrition and metabolism.
