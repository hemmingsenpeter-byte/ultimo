# REL-103 — Intimacy and consent

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Voluntary ongoing consent

- **Outcome:** Explain voluntary ongoing consent in the context of intimacy and consent.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving voluntary ongoing consent; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to consent scenario assessment.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain voluntary ongoing consent from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of voluntary ongoing consent; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `REL-103.L01`

### L02 — Pressure and ambiguity

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish pressure and ambiguity in the context of intimacy and consent.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving pressure and ambiguity; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
