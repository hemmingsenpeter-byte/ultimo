# PHI-107 — Mortality and consciousness

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 5; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Views of mind

- **Outcome:** Explain views of mind in the context of mortality and consciousness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving views of mind; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to philosophy of mind essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain views of mind from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of views of mind; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-107.L01`

### L02 — Empirical and metaphysical questions

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish empirical and metaphysical questions in the context of mortality and consciousness.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving empirical and metaphysical questions; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to philosophy of mind essay.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain empirical and metaphysical questions from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of empirical and metaphysical questions; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHI-107.L02`
