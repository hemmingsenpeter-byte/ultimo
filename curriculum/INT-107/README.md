# INT-107 — Mathematics and science literacy

Draft · Year 3 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain units and ratios in the context of mathematics and science literacy.
- Compare and distinguish linear and exponential models in the context of mathematics and science literacy.
- Apply graph interpretation in the context of mathematics and science literacy.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about quantitative claims in the context of mathematics and science literacy.
