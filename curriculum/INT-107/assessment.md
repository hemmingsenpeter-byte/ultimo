# INT-107 — Practical assessment

Draft; fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Complete a four-part quantitative problem set: convert units and rates for a fictional venue; compare linear and exponential demand models across four periods; interpret a labelled graph or equivalent table; audit a headline with absolute and relative changes, denominators, uncertainty and causal limits. Show your calculations and alternative interpretations.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Units and ratios | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Calculates rates, conversions and percentage comparisons with correct units. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L02: Linear and exponential models | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Distinguishes patterns numerically, models both and limits extrapolation. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L03: Graph interpretation | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Accurately extracts levels and changes and identifies misleading graphical choices. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L04: Quantitative claims | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Reconstructs quantitative claims, states absolute and relative differences and qualifies inference. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quizzes are formative; self-recorded work is not certification.

## Feedback

Provide a fictional artifact, reasoning, assumptions and corrections. Reviewer identifies one strength, one gap and a specific next task. Retest unmet criteria. Equivalent accessible written, spoken, diagrammatic or simulated evidence is accepted. Independent source, subject and learner review required.
