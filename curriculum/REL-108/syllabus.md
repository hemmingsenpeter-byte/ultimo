# REL-108 — Relationships through adversity

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 4; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
- [REL-105 — Conflict repair](../REL-105/syllabus.md)

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Stress and caregiving

- **Outcome:** Explain stress and caregiving in the context of relationships through adversity.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving stress and caregiving; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to family transition plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain stress and caregiving from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of stress and caregiving; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `REL-108.L01`

### L02 — Practical support

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish practical support in the context of relationships through adversity.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving practical support; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to family transition plan.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain practical support from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of practical support; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `REL-108.L02`

### L03 — Autonomy

- **Outcome:** Apply autonomy in the context of relationships through adversity.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving autonomy; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.

### L04 — Specialist assistance

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about specialist assistance in the context of relationships through adversity.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of specialist assistance; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
