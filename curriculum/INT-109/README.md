# INT-109 — Epistemology

Draft · Year 5 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain belief truth and justification in the context of epistemology.
- Compare and distinguish testimony in the context of epistemology.
- Apply theories of knowledge in the context of epistemology.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about confidence in the context of epistemology.

## Reading
- [The Analysis of Knowledge](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/knowledge-analysis/index.html) — Sections on justified true belief, Gettier problems and alternative analyses.
- [Epistemological Problems of Testimony](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/testimony-episprob/) — Discussion of testimonial justification and competing views.
- [Bayesian Epistemology](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epistemology-bayesian/) — Introduction, degrees of belief and updating.
