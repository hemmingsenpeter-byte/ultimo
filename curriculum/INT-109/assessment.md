# INT-109 — Practical assessment

Draft; fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Prepare an epistemic audit of a fictional report about a service disruption. Distinguish belief, truth and justification; map source dependence; compare at least three accounts of knowledge using a potential counterexample; document a justified confidence update and explain when to suspend judgement or take a reversible action.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Belief truth and justification | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Distinguishes belief, truth and justification and recognises epistemic luck. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L02: Testimony | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Maps source dependence and judges verification proportionately. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L03: Theories of knowledge | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Applies multiple accounts to a shared case and explains disagreements without oversimplifying. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |
| L04: Confidence | Defines the central concepts and distinguishes a counterexample | Updates confidence transparently, handles dependence and separates belief from action. | Demonstrates the criterion without prompts in a novel case, justifies limits and revises after delayed feedback. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; no averaging away omissions. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quizzes are formative; self-recorded work is not certification.

## Feedback

Provide a fictional artifact, reasoning, assumptions and corrections. Reviewer identifies one strength, one gap and a specific next task. Retest unmet criteria. Equivalent accessible written, spoken, diagrammatic or simulated evidence is accepted. Independent source, subject and learner review required.
