## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Belief truth and justification

- **Outcome:** Explain belief truth and justification in the context of epistemology.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving belief truth and justification; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to epistemic audit.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain belief truth and justification from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of belief truth and justification; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `INT-109.L01`
