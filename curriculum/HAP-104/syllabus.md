# HAP-104 — Play and leisure

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours. 

## Prerequisites
None; prior learning may be demonstrated.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Recreation versus productivity

- **Outcome:** Explain recreation versus productivity in the context of play and leisure.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving recreation versus productivity; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to leisure design portfolio.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain recreation versus productivity from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of recreation versus productivity; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `HAP-104.L01`

### L02 — Barriers

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish barriers in the context of play and leisure.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving barriers; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
