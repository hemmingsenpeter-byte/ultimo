# SOC-104 — Practical assessment

Draft — qualified review required. Fictional or synthetic evidence only.

## Capstone

Produce a low-stakes fictional practice plan: distinguish perceived confidence and observable skills, design three voluntary practice variants, give specific feedback and retest a novel scenario, and select appropriate support for preference, access, skill and clinical concerns. Provide accessible alternatives and a stop option; no distress induction, exposure treatment or personal disclosure.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 explanation | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Confidence versus skill | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Separates perceived capability, observed skill and contextual barriers and selects task-appropriate support. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L02: Voluntary graded practice | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Designs voluntary, accessible, low-risk task practice with feedback, transfer and clear non-clinical boundaries. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L03: Feedback | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Provides specific, evidence-based feedback, incorporates correction and designs a comparable retest. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |
| L04: Support needs | Defines concepts and explains the contrasting case | Distinguishes preference, access, skill and clinical support needs; selects appropriate, consent-based routes. | Applies independently to a novel fictional case, explains uncertainty and revises following delayed feedback. |

All four criteria require Level 2; no averaging away missing or safety-critical evidence. Level 3 requires delayed independent performance and appropriate reviewer judgement. Quiz scores are formative; no assessment of personal traits, popularity or clinical competence.

## Feedback and boundaries

Identify one strength, one gap and one corrective task; retest unmet criteria using a novel case. Written, oral, visual or simulated alternatives accepted. No disclosure, real-person profiling, coercion, clinical treatment or certification. Independent evidence, qualified subject, cultural, safety and learner review required.
