# SOC-104 — Social confidence

Draft · Year 2 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain confidence versus skill in the context of social confidence.
- Compare and distinguish voluntary graded practice in the context of social confidence.
- Apply feedback in the context of social confidence.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about support needs in the context of social confidence.

- [Mental health at work](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-health-at-work) — Fact sheet, psychosocial risks and organisational interventions; accessed 2026-11-26.
