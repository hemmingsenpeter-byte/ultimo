# FIN-103 — Tax and insurance

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 2; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Tax concepts

- **Outcome:** Explain tax concepts in the context of tax and insurance.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving tax concepts; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to tax and insurance question brief.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain tax concepts from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of tax concepts; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `FIN-103.L01`

### L02 — Insurance pooling

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish insurance pooling in the context of tax and insurance.
