# PHY-101 — Practical assessment

Draft; use private or fictional evidence.

## Capstone

Annotate two ordinary movements using diagrams or suitable public instructional video. Identify planes, joints, external moment, limitations; no loaded or painful demonstration.

## Rubric

| Competency | Level 1 | Level 2 guided evidence | Level 3 independent extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01: Planes of motion | Explains the idea accurately | Identifies plane and joint motion | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L02: Joint anatomy | Explains the idea accurately | explains joint types and structures | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L03: Muscle leverage | Explains the idea accurately | calculates torque with correct moment arm and units | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |
| L04: Movement observation | Explains the idea accurately | separates observation from diagnosis | Repeats this criterion in a novel case without prompts and justifies limitations. |

All four criteria must reach Level 2; do not average away missing or safety-critical criteria. Knowledge checks are formative. Level 3 needs delayed independent evidence and appropriate review. Higher levels require repeated application and qualified judgement.

## Feedback

Submit artifact, assumptions, corrections and reflection privately. Reviewer identifies one strength, gap and next practice action. Retest the unmet criterion. Equivalent accessible written, oral, visual, simulated or supervised evidence; no personal disclosure required. Draft educational framework only. Use fictional cases; no personal diagnosis, prescription, hazardous practice, financial/legal instruction or unqualified certification. Obtain qualified/local review before publication.
