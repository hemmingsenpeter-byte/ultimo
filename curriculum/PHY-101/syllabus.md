# PHY-101 — Anatomy and biomechanics

Framework defined; lesson content: **planned**. Year 1; Level 2 guided practice, Level 3 independent extension; 6 estimated study hours.

## Lesson blueprints

### L01 — Planes of motion

- **Outcome:** Explain planes of motion in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving planes of motion; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to annotated movement map.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain planes of motion from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of planes of motion; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-101.L01`

### L02 — Joint anatomy

- **Outcome:** Compare and distinguish joint anatomy in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving joint anatomy; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Compare two cases or approaches using explicit criteria; identify an important limitation. Connect the result to annotated movement map.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain joint anatomy from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of joint anatomy; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-101.L02`

### L03 — Muscle leverage

- **Outcome:** Apply muscle leverage in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving muscle leverage; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Work through a new case or simulation; document assumptions and steps. Connect the result to annotated movement map.

### L04 — Movement observation

- **Outcome:** Evaluate and justify decisions about movement observation in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.

- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of movement observation; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-101.L04`
