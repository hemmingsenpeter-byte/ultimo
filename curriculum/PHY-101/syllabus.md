## Lesson blueprints and authored materials
### [L01 — Planes of motion](L01.md)

- **Outcome:** Explain planes of motion in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.
- **Example brief:** Use a realistic fictional case involving planes of motion; show reasoning, a contrasting case and limitations.
- **Practice:** Create an annotated explanation with a concrete example and a counterexample. Connect the result to annotated movement map.
- **Knowledge check:** Explain planes of motion from memory and apply it to a different example; state one limitation.
- **Evidence:** Accurate explanation of planes of motion; correct application to a new case; clear rationale and limits.
- **Competency ID:** `PHY-101.L01`
