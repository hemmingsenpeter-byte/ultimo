# PHY-101 — Anatomy and biomechanics

Draft · Year 1 · Level 2 guided practice · 6 indicative study hours, excluding extended practice. Not accredited. [Syllabus](syllabus.md).

## Outcomes
- Explain planes of motion in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.
- Compare and distinguish joint anatomy in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.
- Apply muscle leverage in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.
- Evaluate and justify decisions about movement observation in the context of anatomy and biomechanics.
