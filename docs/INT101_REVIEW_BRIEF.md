## Review record and decisions

For each factual claim record:

```text
Stable lesson ID / reviewed SHA / exact sentence:
Source / edition or publication date / DOI / exact page, section or table:
Evidence type / context / limitations:
Verdict: supported | partial | unsupported | not checked
Correction or additional source needed / change SHA / recheck result:
Reviewer identity and relevant role / date / conflicts of interest:
Outstanding limitations:
```

Check accuracy, context, bias, source quality and intellectual property. Add an appropriate source or narrow/remove unsupported assertions; never invent locators. Keep original fictional exercises distinct from research findings. Record inaccessible full text and unverified claims as gaps.

An independent education, statistics or philosophy specialist, as relevant, should assess outcome alignment, answer feedback, assessment validity and equivalent accessible demonstrations. All four criteria require Level 2 evidence; no averaging away missing criteria. Level 3 requires delayed independent application and documented human judgement. Record gaps in reviewer expertise instead of implying qualified coverage.

Retain draft status until actual evidence satisfies the ordered source, subject, learner and approval gates. Any eventual status change must cite the reviewed version, reviewer decision, remaining limitations and resulting corrections. AI or automated review does not replace required independent judgement; product checks do not approve a course.
