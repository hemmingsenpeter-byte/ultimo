# INT-101 preliminary evidence audit

**AI-assisted preparation, 28 November 2026. Independent human source and subject review remain pending.** This audit does not approve the course, validate its assessments or change its draft status. Canonical lessons and source maps have not been edited as part of the pilot-kit preparation.

Reviewed scope: INT-101's four lessons, companion assessments/source maps and capstone at baseline `5d51751`. A verdict below concerns support from the inspected sources; “unsupported” does not mean a proposition has been disproved. The fragments quoted are from Ultimo, not the research papers.

## Sources inspected

- **D13:** Dunlosky et al. (2013), DOI `10.1177/1529100612453266`, [full text hosted by Pennsylvania State University](https://ritter.ist.psu.edu/ist521/dunloskyRMNW13.pdf), printed pp. 4–58. Locators below use printed pages.
- **RK06:** Roediger and Karpicke (2006), DOI `10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x`, [author-laboratory full text](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2006/2006_Roediger_Karpicke_PsychSci.pdf), printed pp. 249–255.
- **Definitions:** [OpenStax Psychology 2e, section 8.1, Retrieval](https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/8-1-how-memory-functions), and [APA Dictionary, recognition memory](https://dictionary.apa.org/recognition-memory).

RK06 studied university students aged 18–24 recalling two short prose passages, without feedback. Its results concern retention; they do not validate Ultimo's feedback formula, novel application, competency threshold or exact training schedule. See methods pp. 250–252 and discussion p. 254. Full papers remain outside the public repository and pilot archive.

## Review and correction work still required

1. Have an appropriately qualified independent reviewer inspect the cited passages, limitations and full lesson/assessment wording. Record identity, relevant expertise, date, reviewed revision and conflicts of interest. This AI audit does not fill that role.
2. Resolve definitions, scope and original-design labels in the prose; mirror accepted changes in Markdown/JSON answers and feedback. For L02's closed-note question, describe this exercise rather than implying every beneficial retrieval activity must be closed-book.
3. Replace broad paragraph source maps with separate empirical claims, working definitions and original exercises. Record precise locators and unresolved claims, without promoting review status from automated validation.
4. Check accessible alternatives, outcome alignment and rubrics separately. The learner walkthrough and seven-day follow-up provide different evidence; neither should be recorded as completed before actual sessions.
